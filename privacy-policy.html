What information do we collect?

When you contact us through this website, we may receive information that you choose to provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Details of your enquiry
  • Information about the services you are interested in
  • Other information you choose to include in your message

We collect only information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry and provide our services.

How do we use your information?

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Discuss your requirements
  • Provide information about our services
  • Arrange or manage a service you request
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry or service
  • Keep appropriate business records where necessary

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Who may see your information?

Information submitted through the website may be accessed by ERDOGAN BAKERY LIMITED for the purposes of responding to enquiries and providing services.

If we use third-party service providers such as an email or website hosting provider, those providers may process information only to the extent necessary to provide their services.

How long do we keep your information?

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries and maintaining appropriate business records.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or dispose of it securely.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights including:

  • Requesting access to personal information we hold about you
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion of information in certain circumstances
  • Objecting to certain uses of your information
  • Requesting restriction of certain processing where applicable

To exercise your rights or ask questions about your personal information, you can contact us using the details above.

If you believe your personal information has not been handled appropriately, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes to this policy
